Exchange!!! Consolidated some information which might be useful for those going to hanyang for exchange in the future.

Housing

To understand more about housing in Korea, this article provides good information on the different types of housings in Korea. We opted for housing outside as dormitory are not guaranteed. I found the housings for those of us going to hanyang from this site called goshipages. We will be staying at a place called cozytel near hanyang.

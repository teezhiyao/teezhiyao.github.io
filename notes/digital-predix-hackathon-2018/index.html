The Digital Predix Hackathon was held from July 2018 until 20th Sep 2018. Together with Jin Kind and Ryan, we joined the hackathon as Ventus and came out as the winner in the renewable catagory.

About the hackathon

In the first month, we went through the Predix Developer Boot Camp to learn about the predix platform. After the training, we were given a month to work on an application using the microservices on the predix platform. Using relevant data, we used analytics services to come up with a prediction model that is displayed on a predix UI that is created. Github Repo

Learning Experience

Through the hackathon, I got to learn more about industrial IoT and renewables from experts in those fields. Through the app development using the platform, I learnt about things such as APIs (using postman), microservices and predictive analytics.

Ryan, myself & Jin Kind

Special thanks to Harsh & Jessie from GE for being very helpful throughout the entire hackathon
Digital predix hackathon is co-organized by GE(General Electric) & Focustech ventures.

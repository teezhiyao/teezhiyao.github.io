During Summer 2019. I embarked on a 16 weeks internship in Advanced Data Science Center (ADSC) as a Software Engineer Intern.
I developed an eclipse plug-in for XSTAMPP which implements the STLSA process. [XSTAMPP (eXtensible STAMP Platform) is an open-source platform for safety engineering](https://github.com/SE-Stuttgart/XSTAMPP). STLSA Process is a safety engineering model.

### Housing

To understand more about housing in Korea, this [article](https://blog.goshipages.com/housing-faq-ff5f15c7e3a7#.b0dbhd5po) provides good information on the different types of housings in Korea. We opted for housing outside as dormitory are not guaranteed.
I found the housings for those of us going to hanyang from this site called [goshipages](https://goshipages.com/). We will be staying at a place called cozytel near hanyang.

### Courses (Hanyang)

You can find information about the courses in Hanyang [here](https://www.hanyangexchange.com/academics/syllabus/). You can search for more details about those courses [here](https://portal.hanyang.ac.kr/sugang/sulg.do).
A recommended course to take is [Introductory to Korean](https://www.hanyangexchange.com/academics/korean-language/) as a HASS.

For ISTD-related courses, courses we took

| Track | Hanyang | SUTD |
| ----- | ------- | ---- |
| Computer Engineering | ENE4019 Computer Networks | 50.012 Networks |
| Artificial Intelligence | ITE3051 Artificial Intelligence and Application | 50.021 Artificial Intelligence |
| Financial Technology | ITE3016 Database System | 50.043 Database and Big Data |
| Financial Technology | BUS3002 Financial Management | 40.240 Investment Science (ESD) |

Although there is a machine learning theory in the pre-approved course list, it's most likely not offered any more. Therefore, we will most likely have to take Statistical ML from ESD in Term 7.

### Vaccination

Not going recommend what vaccinations to take as it is better to do your own research. However, for Singaporeans, you can check childhood vaccination [here](https://www.nir.hpb.gov.sg/nirp/eservices/login) and [here (NS)](https://www.safhealth.gov.sg/).

The second leg of the trip, and the one I'd planned around.

**May 29-31 — IOI Concerts (Days 1-3)**

Day 1: bought the lightstick. Day 2: bought the shirt. Day 3 (Sunday): last day with a super long encore. The special events organised by the fans were very well done. Three days of concerts, three days of being in a crowd that all came for the same reason — that part doesn't need much more explanation.

**June 1 — Alpaca World + Nami Island**

A Klook tour that was fine overall, but the tour guide had too many filler words (語助詞) when speaking, whether in English or Chinese. It became distracting.

**June 2 — Myeongdong + Nanta Show**

Slept in, then wandered Myeongdong. Caught the Nanta show in the evening — honestly didn't feel it was worth the time or money.

**June 3 — Dance Class + 헤이그**

Went for a dance class with Yes Kpop. Felt a bit underwhelming — maybe because it's catered towards kids? There wasn't much in the way of fixing your moves or detailed instruction.

The musical 헤이그 (Hague) at night was a different story. This was good. Really good. I understood about half the lines, but the storytelling was strong enough that you could follow and infer the rest. Recommended. Didn't go in with high expectations, but left impressed.

추천해요. 너무 신기했어요. 다 이해가 안 됐는데, 그래도 강정적이었어요. 시작 전에 기대가 많이 없었는데, 끝난 후에 배우하고 연기에 대해 연구하게 됐어요. 다음 날 보면 숭배하게 연구하게 돼요. 그런데 휴은 님이도 잘해서요.

**June 4 — Pocheon Tour**

Too many people on this tour, not much vibe. Sat beside another Singaporean lady (Olivia), but she seemed very closed off, so I didn't engage much either. More memorable was the German mother-daughter pair — the mother was quite friendly.

**June 5 — Departure**

Tired at this point. Kept it simple and headed to the airport early.

---

Looking back, the impromptu approach to the trip worked better than I expected. The day tours became an unexpected way to meet people, even if the quality of the experience depended heavily on the guide and group size. Taiwan's smaller tour groups felt noticeably better. And the unplanned moments — overhearing IOI fans on the plane, the conversation with strangers on a tour bus, a musical I walked into without expectations — those ended up being the most memorable parts.

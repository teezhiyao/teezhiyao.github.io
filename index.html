    I try to plan deliberately; without planning, avoidable failure is more likely.

    Everything has a trade-off

    I try to stay aware that every choice has opportunity cost, and I aim to make intentional decisions based on what matters most.

    Be in the arena (The Man in the Arena)

    I try to favour action over passive observation—those who try and risk failure often learn the most.

    I strive for clear, honest communication — listening and respectful responses build mutual trust.

    Vulnerability is strength

    I aim to be open about uncertainty or mistakes because that enables learning and real connection.

    Practice consistency and empathy
